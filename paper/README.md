## Findings

Broad bf16 LoRA SFT improves held-out SVG-Diagrams executable validity from 3.1% to 35.9%, with no reliable final validity gain on either external VFIG subset. Validity and end-to-end fidelity reach half of their observed source-distribution gain at the same 20% checkpoint. A 65-pair candidate diagnostic changes from 19 to 20 strict matches, exposing discrimination that is not consistently expressed through executable generation.

These findings do not support the earlier simple syntax-first hypothesis. Invalid-as-zero fidelity and a training/inference stopping-token mismatch limit interpretation; the manuscript discusses both.
